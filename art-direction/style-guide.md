# Visual Style Guide — The Artificial Republic
Direction: **The Water Reads Back**

## Photography style
Type: Conceptual / atmospheric / painterly (AI-generated, cinematic)
Subjects: Venice at dawn/dusk — the Grand Canal, the low Fabbriche Nuove arcade, repeating stone bays, reflected facades, near-abstract canal water, narrow shadowed rii.
Composition: Wide, contemplative, generous negative space; water as a mirror; facades dissolving into fog.
Lighting: Low sun barely breaking through mist; long shadows; luminous but never crisp.
Color treatment: Desaturated. Dusk-teal shadows, mist highlights, a single warm gold accent. Turner-like.

## Mood & tone
Primary emotion: contemplative, elegiac
Supporting: luminous, weighty, quietly ominous
Energy: calm / contemplative

## Consistency rules
- Every image: fog or haze present, low saturation, film grain, no people, no boats.
- Palette anchors: dusk-teal `#1c2b2e` · mist `#d8d0c2` · gold `#c99a4e` · deep ink `#0e1516`
- Aspect ratio default: 16:9 full-bleed bands.

## Cliché blacklist
- Gondola / postcard / bright-daylight tourist Venice
- HDR, oversaturation, crisp travel-brochure clarity
- Literal AI imagery: chips, robots, glowing brains, circuit overlays, handshakes
- St Mark's Square crowds, pigeons, carnival masks

## AI generation defaults
Suffix: "painterly cinematic atmosphere reminiscent of J.M.W. Turner, heavy dawn fog, desaturated dusk-teal and warm gold palette, fine film grain, low golden light, glassy still-water reflections, no people, no boats, contemplative and elegiac, wide cinematic composition"
